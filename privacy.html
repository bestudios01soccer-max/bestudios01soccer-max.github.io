Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

YT Automation ("the App") is a private tool operated by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. The App has no other users.

Data the App accesses

Through Google OAuth, the App requests the following YouTube scopes:

  • youtube.upload – to upload videos created by the App to the authorised channel;
  • youtube.readonly – to read the authorised channel's name and the processing/privacy status of videos the App uploaded.

How data is used and stored

  • The OAuth refresh token is stored only on the owner's private server and is used solely to perform the actions above.
  • The App stores the IDs, titles and URLs of videos it uploaded, to avoid duplicates and to verify publication.
  • No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

YouTube and Google

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections. After revocation the stored token stops working and is deleted from the server.
